I was lucky enough to attend the [Turing Festival](http://turingfestival.com/) in Edinburgh on 24–25 August 2013, right at the tail end of the [Edinburgh Festival](http://www.edinburghfestivals.co.uk/) - so the city was still buzzing. The talks were diverse and genuinely engaging, with speakers using technology as a foundation rather than the whole subject, which made the event feel far more dynamic than your typical conference.

## What talks did I attend?

- Five Startups Go Mad in Edinburgh
- Future of Money
- Digital Distribution
- Digital Marketing & Growth Hacking
- Festival Keynote by Neal Stephenson
- Future of Music

## Future of money highlights

- [Sandra Alzetta](http://www.iabuk.net/people/sandra-alzetta) from Visa gave a grounded talk on mobile payments and contactless cards, arguing the future of payments is *both* mobile and plastic, each playing an essential role in Visa's vision.
- [Mike Hearn](http://plan99.net/~mike/#top) (Bitcoin, previously Google) delivered one of the festival's real highlights - a wide-ranging talk covering everything from Google's driverless cars to the future of work, painting a picture of autonomous agents running their own businesses in a future "TradeNet" system. Bitcoin came up too, but was almost overshadowed by the bigger ideas around it.

> "Social reviews don't make that much sense," says Mike Hearn of Google. Lots of spam and strangers #turingfest - Andrew Girdwood, 23 August 2013
>
> The future of money by Visa Europe. Trust. Completely agree. Money doesn't do public betas #turingfest - Calum Shepherd, 23 August 2013
>
> Visa Europe forecast 50% of their transactions by 2020 will be mobile #turingfest - Calum Shepherd, 23 August 2013

## Digital distribution highlights

The standout session came from DJ Powers of Valve, who gave insight into the Steam ecosystem and its recent community-contribution developments. Team Fortress 2 was a stand-out success story - a full marketplace built around digital products and community-driven updates, launched to the entire player base. One highlight: a hat seller (for in-game characters) who made six figures from his creations.

> Team Fortress Community Update biggest grossing hour for them after launch. Awesome #turingfest - Calum Shepherd, 23 August 2013

## Digital marketing and growth hacking

Brian Doll from GitHub opened the session with a simple, effective philosophy:

> "Build Something, Tell People"

It sparked some debate - particularly how well it scales inside large organisations - but it's an ideal worth aspiring to regardless, and Brian's use of an animated GIF throughout his talk was a real crowd-pleaser.

JP Rangaswami from Salesforce also gave an inspiring talk. I didn't take notes, but his storytelling as he guided the audience through the shifting technology landscape was genuinely impressive.

> "Living infographics." Steven Drost from Stipso explains how text can become more than a sum of its parts #turingfest - Calum Shepherd, 24 August 2013
>
> Focus on tribes. Focus on communities that already exist - Brian Doll from @github - Calum Shepherd, 24 August 2013

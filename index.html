What I Do

Bring product to life

I partner with teams and customers to build products people love, not just products that work. I care about the experience and the outcome, and I'm increasingly excited by what the AI era now makes possible for customers.

Turn unstructured data into decisions

Whole industries still depend on slow, manual research and curation to produce the documents that decisions rest on. AI is changing that equation, freeing specialists from administration so they can spend their time deciding, not compiling.

Rebuild experiences around real usage

I've led multiple redesigns grounded in usage data and direct user research rather than opinion. One saw a new offer giving legal professionals direct access to property ownership data.

Speaking

Invited to speak at EuroGeographics in Prague, with further talks at Lean Agile Scotland and Lean Agile Glasgow.

Spare time

Outside of work, I'm a serial short-course taker. I'm drawn to anything about human interaction - it's the same skill that makes conversations with customers land, and that helps me really understand what they need.
